Rolling Heart

Private Heart Flow context and factual Heart Data for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Understand the day at a glance

Rolling Heart keeps derived Heart Flow separate from factual Apple Health readings. Explore recent context on iPhone, use Watch-local Heart Flow on Apple Watch, and browse supported complication previews.

Free and optional Premium

The free app includes useful six-hour views, factual Heart Data, supported factual complications, the complete complications gallery, Heart Flow Graph and Lab previews, and the offline Rolling Heart Guide. The one-time Lifetime Premium purchase adds longer ranges, Premium themes, and deeper customization.

Private by design

Health data is processed on your devices. Rolling Heart has no account, ads, analytics, tracking, or developer server, and it does not write Health data.
