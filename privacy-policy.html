Rolling Heart Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 11, 2026

Rolling Heart is an Apple Watch heart-rate visualization app by Brent Andrew O. Chua.

Data Access

Rolling Heart reads heart-rate data from Apple Health only after you grant permission. The app uses this data to show rolling heart-rate charts and Apple Watch complications.

Data Storage and Sharing

Rolling Heart does not create an account, run ads, use analytics SDKs, sell data, or share Health data with third parties. Heart-rate data is read from Health on your device for app functionality.

Rolling Heart does not transmit your Health data to the developer or to a third-party service.

Health Disclaimer

Rolling Heart is for personal wellness and visualization only. It is not intended for medical diagnosis, treatment, monitoring, or emergency use. For medical concerns, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
