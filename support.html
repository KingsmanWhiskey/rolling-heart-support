Rolling Heart Support

Rolling Heart helps you see your recent heart rate at a glance on Apple Watch.

Common Help

  • Make sure Rolling Heart is installed on both iPhone and Apple Watch.
  • Open Rolling Heart on Apple Watch and allow Health access when prompted.
  • If a complication says no recent data, keep wearing your Apple Watch until a new heart-rate reading is available.
  • In the watch face editor, choose one of the three Rolling Heart designs, then set the range to 6h, 12h, or 24h.

Health Disclaimer

Rolling Heart is for personal wellness and visualization only. It is not intended for medical diagnosis, treatment, monitoring, or emergency use.
